Privacy notice

DRAFT — This page needs review and approval by the responsible adult/organization before public registration is enabled. It is an information draft, not legal advice.

Information the service may process

  • Account email and authentication status, managed by Supabase Auth.
  • CartPrac username, permanent account ID, avatar preference, profile visibility and leaderboard visibility.
  • Minecraft UUID and Minecraft username when the user chooses to link a Minecraft account.
  • Aggregate Minecraft statistics synchronized by the CartPrac server: wins, losses, kills and deaths.
  • Forum posts, replies and support-ticket messages created by the user.
  • Uploaded profile image files (PNG, JPEG or WebP, maximum 1 MB).

Service providers

Depending on which features are enabled, information is processed by Supabase (authentication), Cloudflare (Pages static hosting, Workers API/proxy and D1 database infrastructure), Google (optional sign-in) and a transactional email provider when configured; custom SMTP is not yet configured for public email delivery. Each provider has its own privacy terms.

Profile visibility

CartPrac profiles are private by default. A user must opt in before a public profile is shown. Leaderboard participation is a separate setting and is off by default.

Security and retention

Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth; CartPrac does not store plain-text passwords. Verification codes and sessions are managed by the authentication provider. Uploaded avatar files and community content are stored in the configured service databases/storage.

Requests and deletion

Before launch, CartPrac must provide a verified process for users to request account access, correction and deletion, and must specify any records retained for security or moderation.
